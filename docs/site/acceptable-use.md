## The stance

Freeweight rents out open-weight text models. They answer as they were trained. We do not put a second filter in
front of them, we do not read your prompts, and we do not ban anyone for lawful use.

"Uncensored" means that. It does not mean that anything goes. You are responsible for what you ask and for what you
do with the answer, under the law that applies to you.

The always-awake model carries the label "uncensored" on the site. It is an uncensored build: it does not refuse
topics. Everything on this page applies to it in full.

## What is not allowed

We refuse only what the law requires us to refuse, and what would break the service for others.

**Illegal content.**

- Content that sexualises minors. No exceptions, including fiction.
- Any other content that is illegal to produce or possess where you are, or where the service operates.

**Using the service to attack or harm others.**

- Planning or carrying out violence against a person.
- Harassment, stalking or threats aimed at a real person.
- Fraud, scams and impersonation meant to take money or access from someone.
- Building or running malware, or breaking into systems you do not have permission to test.
- Running the research tools against a target in order to attack it. The tools are for reading public information.

**Abusing the service itself.**

- Getting around the limits of the free try.
- Using a key, a session or a balance that is not yours.
- Attacking the gateway, the models or other renters' sessions, or trying to reach the machines behind them.
- Reselling access in a way that hides who is responsible for the use.
- Listing a model you have no right to list, or one built to harm the people who run it.

## What we do not police

- Topics. Adult fiction between adults, dark themes, strong opinions, security research on your own systems, and
  questions about drugs, weapons or medicine are not banned here because they are uncomfortable.
- Tone. The models can be rude, wrong or offensive. That is a property of the model.
- Purpose. We do not ask why you want a model.

## How this is enforced

We do not store prompts or answers, so we do not scan them. We act on what we can see: billing records, abuse of
the free try, traffic that attacks the service, and reports sent to us with evidence.

When this policy is broken we can revoke API keys, end sessions and refuse a wallet. We can remove a listed model.
Where the law requires it, we report illegal content to the authorities.

## Model licences

Every model has a licence, shown on its page. Some licences restrict commercial use or certain uses. Following the
licence is your responsibility.

## Reporting

Report abuse, or a model that should not be listed, to hello@freeweight.xyz. Say what happened and include what you
can show: a session receipt, a transaction hash, a model id.

Report security problems privately to the same address. Do not post the details in public first.
