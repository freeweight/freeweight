A model belongs to a class by its size. The class sets the price.

| Class  | Card class | Price per GPU minute |
| ------ | ---------- | -------------------: |
| Light  | 24 GB      |                $0.03 |
| Medium | 48 GB      |                $0.06 |
| Heavy  | 80 GB      |                $0.10 |

- You pay while your session runs: by the second at the per-minute price, with a one-minute minimum once the model is awake. The wake is free.
- Wake time is not billed.
- On a rented model, tokens are counted on the receipt and never priced.
- One model is always awake and is paid per token: $0.50 per million tokens in, $2.00 per million out, from the same balance. It needs no session. In the chat on the site, 20 short messages a day are free.
- The Heavy class is in preview.
